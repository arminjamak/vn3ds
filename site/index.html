Download

One command on your computer converts a visual novel and sends it to your 3DS, with vn3ds itself the first time. A version that runs right in the browser is on its way.

You need a 3DS with custom firmware and the Homebrew Launcher, on the same Wi-Fi as your computer or with its SD card at hand.

Open this page on your computer. vn3ds converts games on a Mac or Windows PC, then sends them to the 3DS. Copy the link to open it there:

https://arminjamak.github.io/vn3ds/

Nothing to download by hand. The command fetches the converter, converts the game and sends vn3ds and the game together.

Pick your computer.

  1. Get your 3DS ready

    First time: open the Homebrew Launcher and press Y. The command sends vn3ds through it, then your game.

    Already have vn3ds? Open it and press Y.

  2. Open Terminal and paste this Open PowerShell and paste this

    Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal and press Return.

    Press Start, type PowerShell and press Enter.

    curl -fsSL https://arminjamak.github.io/vn3ds/install.sh | sh -s -- your game
    & ([scriptblock]::Create([Text.Encoding]::UTF8.GetString((iwr -useb https://arminjamak.github.io/vn3ds/install.ps1).RawContentStream.ToArray()))) your game
  3. Drag the game in

    Add a space after the command, then drag the visual novel (its folder or the .zip you downloaded) into the window. That pastes its path. Press ReturnEnter.

  4. Leave it running

    It converts the game and sends vn3ds and the game to your 3DS. The game shows up on the vn3ds home screen. Leave the window open until it finishes.

Quick start from source

For developers, or if you'd rather build everything yourself. Everything runs from a terminal on your computer. Type your 3DS's IP address in step 3 and every command below fills it in, ready to copy.

  1. Get the code

    git clone https://github.com/arminjamak/vn3ds && cd vn3ds

    The converter needs Python 3.9 or newer and three packages. ffmpeg is only needed when a game's audio isn't already Ogg Vorbis.

    pip install pillow numpy scipy

  2. Build the engine

    Install devkitPro with the 3DS packages, then build. This produces engine/vn3ds.3dsx.

    sudo dkp-pacman -S 3ds-dev 3ds-zlib 3ds-libogg 3ds-libvorbisidec
    cd engine && make && cd ..
    or

    Download vn3ds.3dsx from Releases and put it in the engine/ folder.

  3. Enter your 3DS's IP

    The commands below show 3DS_IP until you type one.

    Where to find it. Open the Homebrew Launcher and press Y (first time), or open vn3ds: the IP is shown at the bottom of its home screen, e.g. 192.168.1.42.

    Your computer and the 3DS must be on the same Wi-Fi.

  4. First install: engine and demo game

    With the Homebrew Launcher's netloader open (press Y), this sends vn3ds, which starts, saves itself to sdmc:/3ds/vn3ds.3dsx and opens its receiver. Snapshot follows straight after.

    tools/vn3ds-port examples/demo --install --3ds 3DS_IP

    This step uses 3dslink from devkitPro's 3dstools. Without it, use the SD-card route in step 7 once; after that everything goes over Wi-Fi.

  5. Port and send any Ren'Py game

    Keep vn3ds open: it listens in the background, even while you play. Then point the tool at a game. It accepts a project folder, a PC, Linux or Mac build, an Android .apk, a .zip download or loose .rpa archives.

    tools/vn3ds-port path/to/Game --3ds 3DS_IP

    The game shows up on the home screen right away, with a progress bar and the time left, and opens once it's complete. Compiled-only games are decompiled automatically, and port-report.txt lists anything that was skipped or approximated.

  6. Updates later

    Run the same command again. Only changed files are sent, and a dropped connection reconnects and resumes on its own. vn3ds keeps receiving while you play.

    tools/vn3ds-port path/to/Game --3ds 3DS_IP

    To update vn3ds itself, add --install, then restart vn3ds.

    tools/vn3ds-port --install --3ds 3DS_IP

  7. Alternative: SD card

    Power the 3DS off, put its SD card in your computer and run this. It copies the game and the engine onto the card. Faster for very large games.

    tools/vn3ds-port path/to/Game --sd /Volumes/YOUR_SD

    Using the ftpd homebrew app instead? Replace --3ds 3DS_IP with --ftp 3DS_IP:5000.

Your computer's IP

You don't need it for the commands, but it's the quickest way to check that both devices are on the same network. A browser can't read it reliably, so ask your terminal:

macOS
ipconfig getifaddr en0
Linux
hostname -I
Windows
ipconfig

On Windows, look for IPv4 Address under your Wi-Fi adapter. On a Mac, if en0 prints nothing, try en1.

What's inside

vn3ds doesn't run Ren'Py on the console. A converter compiles the game to a small bytecode and 3DS-ready textures, and a native C engine plays it. Owning the renderer is what makes the rest possible.

Stereoscopic depth per layer

Backgrounds sink into the screen, sprites sit at its surface and the textbox floats in front. Tune any tag's depth, or split a picture into several planes.

Handheld-documentary camera

Ren'Py's camera statement with true parallax: slow drift, punch-ins and tilts that slide a scene's layers past each other.

Native minigames, online leaderboard

A story can call a built-in minigame, such as Snake or Invaders, and get its score back. Best scores go to a shared online board.

Wi-Fi transfer while playing

Send a game from your computer in one command. Unchanged files are skipped, dropped connections resume, and the console keeps receiving while you play.

Coverflow home screen

Your games as cards, each showing a snapshot of where you left off. Autosave means you pick up exactly there.

Ports the Ren'Py you write

Labels, menus, ATL transforms, transitions, music and voice, Movie scenes and a useful subset of Python. Tested on games with 12,000+ images.

Snapshot

The bundled demo game, and the showcase for everything above. Its scenes are drawn as layered depth planes, its cast has a full range of expressions, and its chapters weave in the native minigames. It uses every optional engine feature, so it's also the reference for your own vn3ds.json.

It installs with the engine in step 4, or on its own:

tools/vn3ds-port examples/demo --3ds 3DS_IP
